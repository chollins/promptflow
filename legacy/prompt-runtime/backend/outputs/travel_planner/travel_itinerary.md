Here's a personalized itinerary for your visit to Brisbane from September 9-14, focused on recreational activities, using public transport, and maintaining a relaxed pace.

## Day 1: September 9 - Arrival and Exploring South Bank

### Morning Activities
- **Arrival in Brisbane**
- **Check-in at Accommodation** (AUD 150-250)
- **Breakfast at The Coffee Emporium** (South Bank) - Enjoy a relaxed meal with river views. 
  - **Estimated Travel Time:** 30 mins from the airport via City Cat or taxi.

### Afternoon Activities
- **Explore South Bank Parklands**
  - Wander through the gardens, walking paths, and take a ride on the Wheel of Brisbane.
  - **Lunch at South Bank Parklands** - Try one of the food vendors or cafes in the area.
- **Visit Queensland Art Gallery & Gallery of Modern Art (QAGOMA)**
  - Explore the exhibitions at your leisure.
  - **Estimated Travel Time:** 10 mins walking from South Bank.

### Evening Activities
- **Dinner at Aquitaine Brasserie** (South Bank) - Enjoy modern Australian cuisine with river views.
- **Relax at South Bank** - Stroll along the river and take in the vibrant atmosphere.

### Transportation Suggestions
- Use City Cats for a scenic ride along the river.
- Walking is ideal around South Bank.

### Daily Travel Tips
- Take your time exploring the parklands; there are plenty of spots to relax.
- Keep your camera handy for picturesque views.

## Day 2: September 10 - Wildlife and Adventure

### Morning Activities
- **Breakfast at The Little Larder** (near your accommodation)
- **Visit Lone Pine Koala Sanctuary**
  - Cuddle a koala and see other native wildlife.
  - **Estimated Travel Time:** 30 mins by bus from the city center (Route 430).

### Afternoon Activities
- **Lunch at Lone Pine’s café** - Enjoy a casual meal while overlooking the sanctuary.
- **Story Bridge Adventure Climb**
  - Experience climbing the iconic bridge for panoramic views of Brisbane.
  - **Estimated Travel Time:** 20 mins by bus back to city center + 10 mins walking.

### Evening Activities
- **Dinner at The Charming Squire** (near the South Bank) - A gastropub with a great atmosphere.
- **Relax at South Bank** - Enjoy the evening ambiance.

### Transportation Suggestions
- Use the bus for travel to Lone Pine and back.
- Walking from the bus stop to the bridge is easy and scenic.

### Daily Travel Tips
- Book your Story Bridge climb in advance to secure your spot.
- Bring a water bottle and wear comfortable shoes for walking.
