### Personalized Travel Preparation Checklist for Brisbane

#### Essential Documents
- **Passport:** Ensure it’s valid for at least six months beyond your return date.
- **Travel Insurance:** Print a copy of your policy and emergency contact numbers.
- **Flight Itinerary:** Keep a printed copy and a digital version.
- **Accommodation Confirmation:** Print or save a digital copy of your booking details.
- **Local Maps and Guides:** Download offline maps or print relevant pages.
- **Emergency Contacts:** List of important contacts, including local embassy.

#### Clothing Recommendations
- **Daytime Attire:** 
  - Light, breathable fabrics (T-shirts, shorts, dresses) for warm temperatures.
  - Comfortable walking shoes for exploring the city.
- **Evening Attire:** 
  - Smart-casual outfits for dining (e.g., nice shirt, dress pants).
  - Light jacket or sweater for cooler evenings.
- **Swimwear:** If planning to visit beaches or pools.
- **Accessories:** 
  - Sunglasses and a hat for sun protection.
  - A light scarf or wrap for cooler evenings.

#### Electronics and Chargers
- **Smartphone:** Ensure it’s unlocked and compatible with local networks.
- **Camera:** For capturing memories; don’t forget extra batteries or memory cards.
- **Chargers:** For all electronic devices.
- **Power Adapters:** Australia uses Type I electrical sockets (230V).
- **Portable Charger:** For on-the-go charging.

#### Health and Medication Reminders
- **Prescription Medications:** Bring enough for the duration of your stay, plus a few extra days.
- **Over-the-Counter Medications:** Pain relievers, allergy meds, etc., as needed.
- **First Aid Kit:** Basic supplies (band-aids, antiseptic wipes).
- **Sun Protection:** High SPF sunscreen, lip balm with SPF, and after-sun lotion.

#### Currency and Payment Tips
- **Australian Dollars (AUD):** Have some cash on hand for small purchases.
- **Credit/Debit Cards:** Notify your bank of your travel plans to avoid card blocks.
- **Payment Apps:** Consider using local payment apps (like Apple Pay or Google Pay) if accepted.

#### Transportation Reminders
- **Public Transport Card:** Purchase a Go Card for easy access to buses, trains, and ferries.
- **Bike Rental Info:** Research nearby bike rental stations if you plan to cycle.
- **Emergency Transport Options:** Save contact info for local taxis or rideshare services.

#### Local Etiquette Reminders
- **Tipping:** Not mandatory, but rounding up or leaving 10-15% is appreciated for good service.
- **Punctuality:** Aim to be on time for all appointments and events.
- **Casual Attire:** While casual is acceptable, smart-casual is preferred for dining.

#### Things to Do Before Leaving Home
- **Check Flight Status:** Confirm your flight details a day before departure.
- **Secure Home:** Lock all doors and windows, and consider a home security check.
- **Arrange for Pet Care/House Sitting:** If applicable, ensure pets are cared for.
- **Set Out-of-Office Notifications:** For work or email accounts if necessary.

#### Last-Minute Travel Tips
- **Check Weather Forecast:** Pack any last-minute clothing based on the latest forecast.
- **Stay Hydrated:** Drink plenty of water before your flight.
- **Pack Snacks:** For the journey, especially if traveling with children.
- **Keep Essentials Accessible:** Ensure your passport, wallet, and phone are easy to reach during travel.

By following this checklist, you'll be well-prepared for your trip to Brisbane, ensuring a smooth and enjoyable experience! Safe travels!
